    Vacations to Africa

    The second-largest continent in the world, covering 6% of the total surface of the earth, Africa is a world within itself. A huge continent with varied climates, landscapes and cultures, you could visit Africa hundreds of times and never have the same experience.

    The birthplace of humankind, Africa is a land of great ancient civilisations. Here, you can uncover astounding historic sights, from the Great Pyramids of Egypt to the towering obelisks of the Kingdom of Aksum in Ethiopia and the Cradle of Humankind in South Africa. The continent is home to 54 countries and can be roughly divided into five regions: northern, central, eastern, western and southern Africa.
